Supply Chain at Scale: How Decentralized Purchasing Silently Destroys Margins

For organizations that grow through acquisition, purchasing is one of the first casualties of scale. Each acquired entity comes with its own vendor relationships, its own pricing, its own approval processes. In the short term, this is tolerated because there are more pressing integration priorities. In the long term, it becomes one of the most significant sources of margin erosion in the entire business - and one of the hardest to see clearly from the top.
