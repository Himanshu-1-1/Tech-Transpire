**Information you give us when booking a call.** If you book an intro call, you use a scheduling page operated by Microsoft (Microsoft Bookings). - *Server and security logs.* Our hosting provider records technical data for every request, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the date and time. We use it to deliver the Site, prevent abuse (including spam protection on the contact form) and diagnose errors. [[Retention period set by the host — e.g. 30 days.]]
- *Analytics.* [[Choose one and delete the others:]]
  - [[Option A — none: "We do not use analytics or advertising cookies."]]
  - [[Option B — cookieless analytics (e.g. Plausible / Cloudflare Web Analytics): "We use privacy-focused analytics that count visits without cookies and without identifying you."]]
  - [[Option C — Google Analytics 4: "With your consent, we use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to understand how visitors use the Site. You can decline or withdraw consent at any time through [[cookie settings link]]."]]
