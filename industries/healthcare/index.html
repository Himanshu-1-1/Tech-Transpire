Industries

Healthcare

Healthcare organizations change under constraints that make a wrong plan unusually expensive: regulated data, reimbursement rules, compliance deadlines, and growth through acquisition that leaves several systems doing the same job.

The experience on this page comes from engagements in that environment. Each one is labelled with when and by whom it was led.

Situations we have worked in

  1. Compliance-driven migrations under federal warnings

    “A healthcare compliance provider processing regulated patient data on behalf of hospital systems across the United States. Growth through acquisition had left the organization running three separate legacy intake methods, all feeding the same downstream platform.”

    Engagement led by Mack Akhani prior to founding Tech Transpire.

    Two Quarters of Migration Work, Cleared in a Single Day →

  2. Consolidating duplicate processing platforms for a regulated program

    “A healthcare compliance provider processing federally regulated patient survey data on behalf of hospital systems across the United States. A series of acquisitions had left the organization operating multiple sample processing engines, each running the same regulated program on a different platform.”

    Engagement led by Mack Akhani prior to founding Tech Transpire.

    A Seven-Year Migration, Closed in 19 Hours →

  3. Customer implementation in a healthcare e-learning platform

    “A healthcare e-learning platform serving hospital systems across the United States. New customer activation ran across seven departments - Sales, Platform, Product Management and Design, Implementation, Data Integration, Training and Operations - from the moment a contract was marked closed-won in Salesforce through to operations handoff.”

    Engagement facilitated by Mack Akhani, LSSBB, as Lean Transformation Consultant prior to founding Tech Transpire.

    What Value Stream Mapping Found: 9.5 Hours of Work Inside 208 Days of Delays →

  4. Purchasing across a multi-location, PE-backed care network

    “A private equity-backed eye care operator had grown to over 80 clinics and 10 surgery centers through rapid acquisition. Each location was independently negotiating with vendors. No one at the enterprise level had full visibility into pricing variability, inventory levels, or transaction history.”

    Supply Chain at Scale: How Decentralized Purchasing Silently Destroys Margins →

  5. Data loss at an unowned handoff

    “A healthcare client was experiencing data loss that was causing patients to miss Medicare reimbursements. The individual steps in the data intake process were functioning as designed. The failure was happening at the transition between the legacy intake method and the new automated platform - a handoff that nobody owned cleanly, and that had not been fully stress-tested in production conditions.”

    When Processes Break Down, Look at the Handoffs — Not the Steps →
